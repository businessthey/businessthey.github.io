This week focused on establishing the scope of the project, identifying potential research directions, and developing an initial plan for the summer. I met with my advisor to discuss possible project ideas and reviewed prior work related to online safety, platform auditing, and trust and safety systems.

## Activities

- Met with my advisor to discuss potential research directions and summer goals.
- Reviewed DREAM program requirements and project expectations.
- Explored an initial project idea focused on Tinder's Photo Insights feature.
- Discussed the feasibility and scope of evaluating Photo Insights as a summer research project.
- Reviewed an existing project examining Instagram's Teen Account safety systems and discussed how aspects of its methodology could potentially be adapted to dating platforms.
- Began reviewing literature related to online safety, trust and safety systems, and platform auditing.

## Findings

- Evaluating a single feature such as Tinder's Photo Insights would likely require significant technical setup and data collection before meaningful analysis could begin.
- Existing platform auditing methodologies may provide a more practical and structured starting point for evaluating safety systems on dating platforms.
- A broader focus on safety features and auditing frameworks appears more feasible within the timeline of the project.

## Challenges

- Defining a research scope that is both meaningful and achievable within a summer research program.
- Determining whether to focus on a single platform feature or a broader set of safety tools and mechanisms.
- Identifying methodologies that can be adapted to dating platforms while remaining rigorous and practical.

## Next Steps

- Continue reviewing literature on platform safety and auditing methodologies.
- Refine the project's research questions and objectives.
- Identify candidate dating platforms and safety features for analysis.
- Begin developing an initial framework for evaluating and categorizing safety tools across platforms.
