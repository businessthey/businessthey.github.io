August focused on implementing the project's revised methodology and rebuilding the dataset around publicly available platform documentation. After deciding to move away from controlled account testing, the next challenge was determining how to systematically collect a comprehensive set of safety-related materials from dating platforms.

This required revisiting work from earlier in the summer. Rather than beginning with the safety features I had already identified, the revised approach required building a broader dataset of platform documentation and then identifying relevant safety features from within that dataset.

## Activities

- Began implementing the document collection methodology from Ma et al. as a model for systematically identifying platform safety documentation.
- Initially collected materials using platform search functions and other approaches modeled on the reference methodology.
- Worked with an undergraduate research assistant who identified an alternative scraping approach that could collect pages across each platform's public help, support, blog, and newsroom sites.
- Spent significant time debugging and validating the scraping process to improve coverage and ensure we were capturing as complete a set of platform materials as possible.
- Scraped public documentation from Tinder, Bumble, and Grindr.
- Began reviewing the resulting dataset to identify articles relevant to platform safety and the specific safety features described within them.
- Divided the article review and feature-identification process with the undergraduate research assistant.

## Findings

One of the biggest lessons this month was that systematic data collection was more difficult than simply scraping every available page. Platform websites are structured differently, and an automated collection process does not necessarily produce a complete or immediately usable dataset. We had to examine the results, identify gaps and unexpected behavior, and repeatedly adjust the collection process.

The expanded dataset also changed the scale of the project. Earlier feature sourcing had focused on finding known or clearly advertised safety features. The revised approach instead started with the broader universe of platform documentation and worked toward identifying safety features within it. This reduced our dependence on already knowing what features to search for and created a more systematic process for identifying features that might otherwise have been missed.

The process also reinforced the value of including multiple types of official platform communication. Help and support pages often explain how existing features operate, while newsroom and blog posts provide information about feature launches, updates, availability, and the ways platforms describe their intended safety benefits.

## Challenges

This month required essentially redoing a significant portion of the data collection completed earlier in the summer. Although the initial feature inventory remained useful for understanding the landscape, it was not collected using the more systematic methodology the project had now adopted.

The scraping process also required substantially more debugging and validation than expected. Collecting a large number of URLs did not automatically mean that we had captured every relevant page or that every collected page was useful for the research.

Finally, the expanded corpus created a new challenge of scale. With thousands of pages across the three platforms, we needed a consistent process for determining which materials were relevant and extracting safety features from them without losing the methodological consistency that motivated the pivot in the first place.

## Next Steps

- Continue reviewing the collected Tinder, Bumble, and Grindr documentation.
- Apply consistent criteria for determining whether each page is relevant to platform safety.
- Identify and document safety features described within relevant pages.
- Continue dividing and coordinating article review with the undergraduate research assistant.
- Standardize feature names when multiple platform documents describe the same or closely related functionality.
- Continue validating the completeness of the collected dataset.
- Begin organizing the identified features for comparison across Tinder, Bumble, and Grindr.
